Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

This site is the personal portfolio of Aakash Sethi. The policy below covers what data is collected when you visit, how it's used, and how you can opt out.

1. What I collect

  • Anonymous analytics (only if you accept the cookie banner) — page views, referrer, approximate location (city-level via IP, with IP anonymization on), device type, and time on page. Collected via Google Analytics 4.
  • Contact form submissions — your name, email, LinkedIn URL (if provided), and the answers you give in the intake form. Stored only in the email I receive and the inbox the form sends to.
  • Newsletter subscribers — your email address, stored at Buttondown (the newsletter provider). Nothing else.

2. What I do NOT collect

  • No advertising cookies, ad personalization, or remarketing pixels.
  • No third-party tracking beyond Google Analytics (consent-gated).
  • No identification of anonymous visitors. I cannot see who individually visits this site — only aggregate trends.

3. Cookies

Analytics cookies are denied by default. Nothing is set until you click Accept in the cookie banner. If you accept, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (typically _ga, _ga_*) to count unique visitors over time.

You can change your mind below — clearing your consent will hide the cookie banner again on next visit so you can re-choose.

4. How I use this data

  • Analytics — to understand which pages or posts get attention, and which content is worth writing more of. Aggregate trends only.
  • Contact form — to evaluate whether a consulting or coaching engagement is a fit, and to respond.
  • Newsletter — to send you posts when I publish them. That's the only use.

5. Who I share data with

I don't sell or share your data. The third parties involved in processing this site are limited to:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous traffic data, only if you opt in.
  • Buttondown — your email address, only if you subscribe to the newsletter. Their privacy policy.
  • Mailtrap — used to deliver contact form submissions to my inbox.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this site. They keep standard server logs.

6. Your rights

You can:

  • Decline analytics cookies on first visit, or reset your choice above.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time — every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Email me at aakash.sethi7@gmail.com to request deletion of any data you've submitted (contact form responses, newsletter subscription, etc.).

7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If the policy changes substantively, the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect that. For meaningful changes, newsletter subscribers will get a heads-up.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: aakash.sethi7@gmail.com
